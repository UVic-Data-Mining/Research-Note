# Project Work Log - Milestone 1

## Work / Meeting Details

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| **Ideas Discussed** | Discussed whether existing phishing URL detection systems can reliably detect AI-generated phishing-like URLs and what URL characteristics may be associated with successful non-detection. |
| **Research / Study** | Reviewed the growing use of AI in phishing attacks and their potential economic impact. We also examined previous work on phishing URL detection and AI-generated phishing URLs to refine our research direction. |
| **Implementation** | Prepared and refined the Milestone 1 proposal. |
| **Issues / Problems** | The initial topic of AI-generated phishing detection was too broad. We needed to narrow the scope and distinguish our project from existing research on classifier performance and generalization. |

## Meeting Outcomes

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| **Decisions** | We decided to focus on identifying URL characteristics associated with the non-detection of AI-generated phishing-like URLs by an existing phishing classifier, rather than only comparing detection performance. |
| **Next Steps** | Review and categorize existing research related to phishing URL detection and AI-generated phishing attacks, compare representative approaches, and identify where our project fits within the existing literature. |
